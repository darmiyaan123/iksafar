Refund & Cancellation Policy

Understand our policy regarding cancellations and refund processing for tour packages.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Overview

At Iksafar Travel, we strive to provide the best possible independent holiday packages. However, we understand that unexpected situations can arise, requiring you to cancel or modify your travel plans.

2. Cancellation Charges

If you wish to cancel your booked tour package, cancellations must be submitted in writing via email to ankit@iksafar.com or hr@iksafar.com. Standard cancellation slabs apply as follows:

  • 30 days or more before departure: 25% of the total package cost.
  • 15 to 29 days before departure: 50% of the total package cost.
  • Less than 15 days before departure: 100% (No refund).

3. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed within 7 to 10 business days through the original mode of payment or bank transfer.

4. Contact Us

For any refund claims or questions, feel free to call us at +91 8882911056.
